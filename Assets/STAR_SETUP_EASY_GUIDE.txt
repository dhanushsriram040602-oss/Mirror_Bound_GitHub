═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  ⭐ SIMPLE STAR SYSTEM SETUP - FOLLOW THESE EXACT STEPS ⭐
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎯 DO THIS FOR EVERY LEVEL SCENE (Level_1, Level_2, etc.)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1: Open Your Level
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. In Project window, go to: Assets → Scenes
2. Double-click "Level_1.unity" to open it

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2: Add Star System (Takes 5 seconds!)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. At the TOP of Unity, click: Tools
2. Click: Star Rating System
3. Click: Add to Current Scene

✅ You'll see "StarRatingSystem" appear in Hierarchy!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3: Set Time Limits (In Inspector)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. In Hierarchy, click "LevelSettings"
2. Look at Inspector on the right
3. Find these numbers and change them:
   
   Three Star Time: 30
   Two Star Time: 60

   (You can make them easier or harder later!)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 4: Connect Time Display (One time only!)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. In Hierarchy, expand: Canvas → WinPanel
2. You should see "Time text" 
3. In Hierarchy, click "Canvas"
4. In Inspector, find "Win Menu" component
5. Find empty box that says "Time Text"
6. DRAG "Time text" from Hierarchy into that box

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 5: Save and Test!
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Press Ctrl+S (or Cmd+S on Mac) to save
2. Press the PLAY ▶️ button at the top
3. Complete the level FAST (under 30 seconds)
4. You should see:
   - 3 yellow stars ⭐⭐⭐
   - "Time: X.XXs" text

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING - If it's NOT working:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: Still getting 1 star even when fast
Fix: Open Window → General → Console and check for RED errors

Problem: Time shows 0.00s
Fix: Make sure you did Step 2 (Add StarRatingSystem)

Problem: No stars showing at all
Fix: In Hierarchy, click Canvas → Find WinMenu component
     → Check that Star Images array has 3 items

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
✅ COPY TO OTHER LEVELS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

After Level_1 works:

1. Open Level_2
2. Do Step 2 again (Add StarRatingSystem)
3. Adjust time limits in Step 3
4. Done! (Time text is already connected)

Repeat for all your levels!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

That's it! If you follow these 5 steps, stars will work! 🌟
